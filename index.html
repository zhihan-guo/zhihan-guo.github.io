About

Hi. This is Zhihan GUO (郭芷含). I am a final year Ph.D. candidate at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering in The Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Professor Irwin King. I received my M.S. degree from The University of Hong Kong in 2022, and my B.S. degree from the Beijing Institute of Technology in 2021.

My research generally focuses on resource-constrained domain-specific federated LLM systems, and AI4Green.
